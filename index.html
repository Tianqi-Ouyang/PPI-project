PPI → AKI after ICI

New-user cohort, time-varying Cox model, 730-day horizon

The question

Among patients who were not already on a proton-pump inhibitor when they started immune checkpoint inhibitor therapy, does starting a PPI raise the hazard of acute kidney injury over the following two years?

Design

  • Cohort — 8,911 patients with no PPI at or before ICI start and at least one creatinine drawn afterwards.
  • Outcome — AKI, computed from each patient’s serial creatinine after the ICI start date (KDIGO-style: a creatinine 1.5× the rolling 90-day median, or 0.3 mg/dL above the rolling 2-day minimum).
  • Exposure — PPI, time-varying. Everyone is unexposed at ICI start (day 0); a patient who fills a PPI on day k becomes exposed from day k, so their pre-PPI follow-up counts as unexposed person-time.
  • Follow-up — to the earliest of AKI, last creatinine, death, or 730 days.
  • Model — time-varying Cox, Surv(tstart, tstop, AKI) ~ PPI.

What the diagnostics show

The headline time-varying Cox estimate is large and highly significant, but it does not survive inspection as a drug effect:

  • Mirror window — AKI incidence in the week before the first PPI (6.05 per 1,000 person-days) is essentially the same as the week after (5.98), and peaks on the prescription day itself (14.43). A drug cannot raise risk before it is taken.
  • Time since initiation — the hazard decays from 9.77 in the first week to 0.99 (0.73–1.33) beyond one year of continued exposure. A nephrotoxin does not become harmless the longer it is taken.
  • Induction ladder — 2.57 → 1.85 → 1.60 → 1.36 at 0/30/90/180-day lags, monotone with no plateau.
  • Death negative control — from day 8 onward, starting a PPI predicts death more strongly than it predicts AKI (e.g. 3.12 vs 2.49 at days 31–90).

Together these point to the acute clinical episode in which the PPI is ordered — which also drives the creatinine sampling that makes AKI detectable — rather than to the drug.

Data privacy

EMPI, MRN, names and dates of birth are used as local join keys only and never appear in rendered output. data_management.qmd is not executed at render time — it reads RPDR extracts that stay local. analysis.qmd renders from cache/qc.rds, which is aggregate-only. Cells below 11 are suppressed.

Data sources (local only, not in this repo)

  • Final cohort/final_master_0114_raw.xlsx — ICI-CKD analytic master
  • RPDR _Lab.txt — serial creatinine (AKI outcome, end of follow-up)
  • RPDR _Med.txt — PPI dispensings (baseline exclusion + initiation date)
